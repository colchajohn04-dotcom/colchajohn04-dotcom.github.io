01Automated Drink Mixer

Objective

Develop a multi-axis Cartesian system that positions an end-effector over cups and presses flavor pods to dispense liquid.

Engineering approach

The machine combined X–Y positioning with a vertically mounted actuator reused from an earlier syringe-dispensing project. The team moved from foamboard to 20 × 20 mm aluminum extrusion for increased rigidity.

My contribution

I cut and tapped aluminum extrusion for the frame and designed and laser-cut an MDF housing for the LCD screen and physical selection buttons. Teammates developed the cup holders, leveling components, and interface software.

Result & learning

This project connected mechanism design, structural fabrication, electronics packaging, and team integration in one functional prototype.

02Motorized Cart

Objective

Build a motorized wheeled cart with a target travel distance of 10 feet and evaluate the effects of acceleration on payload stability.

Engineering approach

The assembly used a wooden chassis, 5-inch wheels, 3D-printed support parts, a DC motor, an L298N motor driver, and a belt-and-pulley drive.

My contribution

I laser-cut the main chassis plate and wooden wheels. The original report also describes CAD and fabrication work on a motor holder and axle supports; I'll confirm the exact division of contributions before publishing a more specific claim.

Result & learning

The team combined fabrication, transmission design, wiring, and tipping-risk calculations. A preliminary motion test demonstrated mobility; the archived page does not document a verified full 10-foot run.

03Foamboard Plotter

Objective

Build a low-cost, two-axis plotter that reproduces programmed shapes.

Engineering approach

Foamboard carriages moved along X and Y; a motorized pen lifter provided an additional lifting action. An Arduino Mega 2560 interpreted G-code for coordinated motion.

My contribution

Participated in mechanical assembly, motor wiring, calibration, and physical testing of the plotter.

Result & learning

A demonstration recorded the system drawing a square, connecting stepper control and G-code to manufacturing automation concepts.

04Syringe Actuator

Objective

Develop an actuator capable of pushing a syringe plunger against the resistance of fluid dispensing.

Engineering approach

The mechanism built on the earlier pen lifter but required stronger retention and attention to torque and structural stability.

My contribution

Contributed to the CAD design, 3D-printed prototype assembly, and physical load testing.

Result & learning

Demonstrated how different loads change design requirements even when two devices use the same motion principle.

06Tabletop Phone Stand

Objective

Support a phone in portrait and landscape orientation with its case on, while preserving microphone and speaker access.

Engineering approach

After sketching concepts, the stand was modeled in Onshape and hollowed in non-load-bearing regions to reduce printing material.

My contribution

Developed the geometry and evaluated stability, practical access, and manufacturability.

Result & learning

Practiced converting product requirements into a single-piece printable form.

07Four-Bar Linkage Simulation

Objective

Model a mechanism with ground, crank, coupler, and rocker links, then investigate its motion.

Engineering approach

Established joints, applied rotary motion, traced a point path, and explored motion-response data.

My contribution

Assembled and analyzed the linkage digitally as part of a kinematics and dynamics exercise.

Result & learning

Established a workflow for studying paths, velocity, torque, and reaction forces before fabricating more complex systems.
